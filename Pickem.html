Draft Versus

A fresh squad is shuffled together every game. Snake-draft teammates onto your card, category by category — but their rating stays hidden until you commit. Best average wins.

VS
  • 1A random 12-player squad is shuffled together from the whole club before every draft — never the same pool twice.
  • 25 rounds, 5 random categories (Speed, Shooting, Strength, whatever comes up). One category revealed per round.
  • 3Snake draft: you alternate who picks first each round. Once a player's taken, they're off the board for both of you.
  • 4A player's rating in the round's category stays hidden until after you draft them — no scanning for the highest number.
  • 520 seconds per pick. Run out the clock and we'll auto-pick a player for you.
  • 6Your score for a round is that player's rating in that round's category. Add up all 5, divide by 5 — highest average wins.
